About HolidayMaximizer

HolidayMaximizer helps people turn a limited annual-leave allowance into longer breaks by combining leave days with weekends and supported public holidays.

The site is designed for international use. It distinguishes national and regional calendars where possible, explains employment-law caveats, and links to official sources on the country guides that have been verified.

HolidayMaximizer is operated by Kattegat Studio, a Danish enkeltmandsvirksomhed. Business and legal information.

We welcome corrections at contact@kattegatstudio.dk.

What we publish and maintain

The dated guides connect the actual calculator output to its holiday inputs: which days to request, which dates are already off, and how the total is counted. Calendar assumptions and official references sit beside the examples so you can check the result against your own work pattern.

We distinguish a legal leave entitlement from a planning assumption. Where calendar coverage is incomplete, the directory remains available without presenting a generic entry as a fully reviewed guide. Corrections can be sent through our contact page; include the country, region, year and an authoritative source.
