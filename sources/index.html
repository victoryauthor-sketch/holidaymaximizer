Sources & data methodology

HolidayMaximizer separates public-holiday calendars from employment-law annual-leave context. The global ranking uses a normalized annual-leave value plus a conservative public-holiday baseline; exact dated holidays remain a separate calendar dataset.

Public-holiday calendars

Selected regional calendars are stored locally. Broader country and first-level subdivision coverage uses the Nager.Holidays Community API v4 ↗. For years or subdivisions not stored locally, the planner requests the API in the browser and caches successful responses in local storage to avoid unnecessary repeat requests.

Verified annual-leave sources

Every country in the verified ranking now has a comparable five-day-week value. Weeks are converted to five workdays; statutory six-day counts are normalized to five; calendar-day leave periods use a 5/7 ratio. The normalized number is a comparison aid, not a replacement for the legal wording.

Public-holiday benchmark in the ranking

The ranking uses a conservative numeric public-holiday baseline so every row can be sorted without depending on a live API. Where states, provinces, cantons or nations differ, the lower/common figure is used and the row is labelled accordingly. Exact year-specific dates in the planner come from the locally curated calendars and Nager.Holidays Community API v4. Public holidays do not always create a statutory paid day off for every private-sector worker.

Corrections

Employment law and holiday calendars change. If you find an error, email contact@kattegatstudio.dk with the country, year and an official source.

Global annual-leave coverage

HolidayMaximizer prefers national legislation and official labour authorities. To avoid blank comparisons for countries whose primary law has not yet been encoded, the comparison/ranking can use a clearly labelled secondary cross-country statutory benchmark. Territories and special jurisdictions are never assigned a parent-country entitlement automatically.

2027 worked-guide calendar references

The following sources were checked on 2026-10-17 for the calendar assumptions used in the ten worked guides. Calendar rules and leave entitlement are separate: a holiday date does not guarantee that every worker is off.

  • Ireland calculations — Workplace Relations Commission. National calendar.
  • Germany calculations — Berlin.de. Berlin example.

  • Netherlands calculations — Government.nl. Bundled planning calendar.
  • Italy calculations — Italia.it. National calendar.
  • United States calculations — U.S. Office of Personnel Management. Federal observed calendar, Monday–Friday schedule.
  • Austria calculations — Austrian Foreign Ministry holiday calendar. National calendar.
  • Belgium calculations — Belgian FPS Employment. National dates before employer replacement days.
