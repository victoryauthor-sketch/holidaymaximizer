What does “maximizing leave” mean?

Choose a country, the region where you work, a year, the days you can book and your preferred trip length. The planner counts each ordinary Monday–Friday workday as leave, then joins it to weekends and the selected calendar’s public holidays.

The multiplier is consecutive days off divided by leave days booked. It compares the efficiency of a break; it does not increase your legal leave entitlement. Results are independent alternatives, each within the allowance you entered.

A real example: Ireland, 2027

4 leave days → 9 consecutive days off

Book 30 March–2 April 2027. With Easter Monday on 29 March and the weekends of 27–28 March and 3–4 April, your break runs 27 March–4 April.

9 days = 4 leave days + 1 public holiday + 4 weekend days. Good Friday is not assumed off in Ireland. See Ireland’s dates and local rules.

What can change your result?

A different region, employer holiday policy, trip length or leave allowance can change the best dates. The model assumes Saturday–Sunday weekends and that selected holidays are days off. It does not model shift work, school holidays, travel prices or employer approval.

Current-year suggestions start today or later. A year-end break can use leave from two different allowance years. Check the exact booking dates and your employer’s calendar before requesting leave.
