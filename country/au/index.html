Australia: make your 2027 leave go further

Exact workdays to book, the public holidays behind each result, and non-overlapping 10-day and 20-day annual schedule plans.

Australia Day falls on Tuesday 26 January 2027 (a 4-day weekend for 1 leave day on Monday 25 January), Easter runs 26–29 March, and substitute days for Christmas and Boxing Day fall on Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December 2027.

Statutory leave, public holidays & local rules: Australian public holidays are declared at the state and territory level alongside the National Employment Standards (minimum 4 weeks paid annual leave for full-time workers). Statutory leave vs. public holidays, bank closures & replacement days

Statutory paid annual leave baseline: 4 weeks (20 days for a 5-day worker). Statutory annual leave is distinct from public holidays: public holidays are calendar dates designated by law or collective agreement, whereas annual leave is your personal bookable allowance.
