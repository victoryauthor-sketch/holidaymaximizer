Australia: make your 2027 leave go further

Exact dates to book, the holidays behind each result, and full-year schedule examples for your leave allowance.

Australia Day falls on Tuesday 26 January 2027 (a 4-day weekend for 1 leave day on Monday 25 January), Easter runs 26–29 March, and substitute days for Christmas and Boxing Day fall on Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December 2027.

Before using these dates: Australian public holidays are legislated at the state and territory level. These examples use New South Wales; dedicated guides for Victoria and Queensland are linked below and selectable in the planner.
