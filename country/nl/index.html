Exact workdays to book, the public holidays behind each result, and non-overlapping 10-day and 20-day annual schedule plans.

King’s Day falls on Tuesday 27 April 2027, followed the next week by Liberation Day (Wednesday 5 May) and Ascension Day (Thursday 6 May) for workers whose CAO grants Liberation Day off.

Statutory leave, public holidays & local rules: Under Dutch law, there is no automatic statutory right to a paid day off on a public holiday; your collective labour agreement (CAO) or employment contract determines which holidays are off. This calendar includes King’s Day (27 April), Liberation Day (5 May) and Ascension Day (6 May), and excludes Good Friday (often worked in the private sector). Verify your CAO before booking.

Statutory leave vs. public holidays, bank closures & replacement days

Statutory paid annual leave baseline: 4 × weekly working hours (20 days for a 5-day worker). Statutory annual leave is distinct from public holidays: public holidays are calendar dates designated by law or collective agreement, whereas annual leave is your personal bookable allowance.
