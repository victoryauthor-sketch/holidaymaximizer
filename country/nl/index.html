King’s Day falls on Tuesday 27 April 2027, followed the next week by Liberation Day (Wednesday 5 May) and Ascension Day (Thursday 6 May) for workers whose CAO grants Liberation Day off.

Before using these dates: A public holiday does not automatically entitle you to time off. This calendar assumes Liberation Day is off but excludes Good Friday; check your CAO or contract before using those results.
