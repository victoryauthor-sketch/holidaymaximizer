How to make your leave go further

Public-holiday planning

  • Chinese New Year is one of the strongest recurring opportunities, especially when adjacent to a weekend.
  • Hari Raya, Deepavali and Christmas can also create useful clusters depending on the year.
  • The statutory annual-leave floor is service-linked, so enter your actual contractual leave if it is higher.

Legal basis and comparison method

Employees covered by the Employment Act who have worked at least three months receive a service-linked minimum starting at seven days.

Regional and calendar caveats

The planner combines weekends with supported public-holiday data and can use first-level regional calendars where the dataset provides them. Employer-specific, municipal and sector-specific days can still differ.
