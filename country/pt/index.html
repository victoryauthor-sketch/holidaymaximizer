How to make your leave go further

Public-holiday planning

  • Freedom Day (25 April), Labour Day, Portugal Day and the December holidays are useful fixed dates to test.
  • Some municipal holidays add local opportunities that a national calendar will not capture.
  • A 22-working-day statutory minimum provides a solid base for multiple optimized breaks.

Legal basis and comparison method

Workers are entitled to at least 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Special accrual rules apply in the first year of employment.

Regional and calendar caveats

The planner combines weekends with supported public-holiday data and can use first-level regional calendars where the dataset provides them. Employer-specific, municipal and sector-specific days can still differ.
