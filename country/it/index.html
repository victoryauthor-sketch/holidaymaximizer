Italy: make your 2027 leave go further

Exact workdays to book, the public holidays behind each result, and non-overlapping 10-day and 20-day annual schedule plans.

Epiphany falls on Wednesday 6 January 2027, Republic Day on Wednesday 2 June, St Francis of Assisi on Monday 4 October, and Immaculate Conception on Wednesday 8 December—creating strong mid-week “ponte” bridges throughout 2027.

Statutory leave, public holidays & local rules: Local municipal patron-saint holidays (such as 29 June in Rome or 7 December in Milan) are not included in the national baseline. The calendar includes 4 October, the restored national public holiday for St Francis of Assisi effective from 2026 under Law 151/2025. In Italy, public holidays falling on Sunday usually confer additional pay (festività non goduta) rather than a replacement Monday off.

Statutory leave vs. public holidays, bank closures & replacement days

Statutory paid annual leave baseline: At least 4 weeks. Statutory annual leave is distinct from public holidays: public holidays are calendar dates designated by law or collective agreement, whereas annual leave is your personal bookable allowance.

Official statutory leave source: Italy — Ministry of Labour and Social Policies. Open official guidance ↗
