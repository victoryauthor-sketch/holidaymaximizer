Belgium: make your 2027 leave go further

Exact workdays to book, the public holidays behind each result, and non-overlapping 10-day and 20-day annual schedule plans.

Ascension Day (Thursday 6 May 2027), Whit Monday (17 May), Belgian National Day (Wednesday 21 July) and Armistice Day (Thursday 11 November) anchor Belgium’s highest-value weekday combinations.

Statutory leave, public holidays & local rules: Under Belgian law, when one of the 10 statutory public holidays falls on a Sunday or a normal non-working day (such as Saturday on a Mon–Fri schedule — in 2027: 1 May, 15 August and 25 December), it must be replaced by a working day. Because joint committees or employers often fix replacement days onto bridge Fridays (such as Friday 7 May after Ascension), this model does not guess those dates: check your employer’s posted replacement-day notice.

Statutory leave vs. public holidays, bank closures & replacement days

Statutory paid annual leave baseline: Up to 4 weeks (normally 20 days on a 5-day schedule). Statutory annual leave is distinct from public holidays: public holidays are calendar dates designated by law or collective agreement, whereas annual leave is your personal bookable allowance.
