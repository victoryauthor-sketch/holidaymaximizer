Statutory leave vs. public holidays, bank closures & replacement days

Statutory paid annual leave baseline: 5.6 weeks (28 days for a 5-day worker; bank holidays may be included). Statutory annual leave is distinct from public holidays: public holidays are calendar dates designated by law or collective agreement, whereas annual leave is your personal bookable allowance.
