How to make your leave go further

Public-holiday planning

  • Easter Monday, Corpus Christi, All Saints’ Day and the Christmas period are recurring opportunities.
  • Corpus Christi always falls on a Thursday, often making Friday a high-value leave day.
  • The statutory leave floor rises from 20 to 26 days once qualifying service reaches ten years.
