United States: make your 2027 leave go further

Exact workdays to book, the public holidays behind each result, and non-overlapping 10-day and 20-day annual schedule plans.

In 2027, OPM observed federal holidays include Friday 18 June (Juneteenth observed), Monday 5 July (Independence Day observed), Thursday 11 November (Veterans Day), Thursday 25 November (Thanksgiving) and Friday 24 December (Christmas Day observed).

Statutory leave, public holidays & local rules: The United States has no federal statutory paid vacation minimum for private-sector workers, and federal holidays apply by law only to federal employees. Private employers decide which holidays are paid days off (for example, many private employers grant the Friday after Thanksgiving off while not observing Columbus Day or Veterans Day). Check your employer handbook.
