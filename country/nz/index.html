How to make your leave go further

Public-holiday planning

  • Easter, ANZAC Day and the King’s Birthday weekend are recurring opportunities.
  • Matariki adds another movable public holiday that can improve a leave plan.
  • Provincial anniversary days may matter depending on where you work.

Legal basis and comparison method

Employees become entitled to at least four weeks of paid annual holidays after 12 months of continuous employment. New legislation is due to replace the current Holidays Act in 2028.

Regional and calendar caveats

The planner combines weekends with supported public-holiday data and can use first-level regional calendars where the dataset provides them. Employer-specific, municipal and sector-specific days can still differ.
