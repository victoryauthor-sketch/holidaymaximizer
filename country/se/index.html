Epiphany falls on Wednesday 6 January 2027 (allowing a 9-day break for 4 leave days), Easter combines Good Friday and Easter Monday (26–29 March), and Ascension Day falls on Thursday 6 May.

Before using these dates: Many Swedish collective agreements grant half-days or de facto days off on Midsummer Eve, Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve, plus “klämdagar” (squeeze days). Confirm your agreement before booking.
