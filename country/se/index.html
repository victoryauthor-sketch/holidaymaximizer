Sweden: make your 2027 leave go further

Exact workdays to book, the public holidays behind each result, and non-overlapping 10-day and 20-day annual schedule plans.

Epiphany falls on Wednesday 6 January 2027 (allowing a 9-day break for 4 leave days), Easter combines Good Friday and Easter Monday (26–29 March), and Ascension Day falls on Thursday 6 May.

Statutory leave, public holidays & local rules: Sweden’s Annual Leave Act (Semesterlagen) provides at least 25 vacation days per year. Many collective agreements also treat Midsummer Eve, Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve as de facto paid days off and grant “klämdagar” (squeeze days such as the Friday after Ascension). Statutory leave vs. public holidays, bank closures & replacement days
