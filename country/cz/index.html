How to make your leave go further

Public-holiday planning

  • Easter Monday, May Day, Liberation Day, the July state holidays and Christmas are key dates to test.
  • When fixed-date holidays fall on Tuesdays or Thursdays, a single leave day can create a four-day break.
  • Some public-sector and education employees have longer statutory leave than the four-week general minimum.

Legal basis and comparison method
