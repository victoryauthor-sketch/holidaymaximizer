Norway: make your 2027 leave go further

Exact workdays to book, the public holidays behind each result, and non-overlapping 10-day and 20-day annual schedule plans.

Easter 2027 in Norway includes Maundy Thursday (25 March), Good Friday (26 March) and Easter Monday (29 March), turning 3 leave days (22–24 March) into 10 consecutive days off, and Constitution Day + Whit Monday both fall on Monday 17 May 2027.

Statutory leave, public holidays & local rules: Norway’s Holiday Act (Ferieloven) expresses statutory leave as 25 working days (virkedager) including Saturdays (= 4 weeks + 1 day, or ~21 Mon–Fri workdays), while most collective agreements grant 5 weeks (30 virkedager = 25 Mon–Fri workdays). Statutory leave vs. public holidays, bank closures & replacement days

Statutory paid annual leave baseline: 25 statutory working days where Saturdays count (4 weeks + 1 day). Statutory annual leave is distinct from public holidays: public holidays are calendar dates designated by law or collective agreement, whereas annual leave is your personal bookable allowance.
