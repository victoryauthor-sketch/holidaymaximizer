Norway: make your 2027 leave go further

Exact dates to book, the holidays behind each result, and full-year schedule examples for your leave allowance.

Easter 2027 in Norway includes Maundy Thursday (25 March), Good Friday (26 March) and Easter Monday (29 March), turning 3 leave days (22–24 March) into 10 consecutive days off.

Before using these dates: Norway’s statutory holiday law counts 25 working days including Saturdays (4 weeks + 1 day, or 5 weeks under most collective agreements). Maundy Thursday and Good Friday are both full public holidays.
